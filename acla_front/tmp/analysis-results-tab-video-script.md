# Kestrel — Lap Results

Approximate runtime: 3 minutes  
Format: Screen walkthrough with first-person voice-over

## Before recording

[Prepare a live session with at least two analyzed baseline laps from the same car and track to demonstrate page navigation, and a result with an available Driver vs Expert trajectory replay. Prefer a result with section timing, throttle, brake, gear, and positioned analysis labels. Check that Replay is enabled. Open Analysis Results and select Lap Results before recording. Start with the result cards collapsed. Use the actual labels and values shown in the recording.]

## Opening: from lap time to driving behavior

[Start on Lap Results with the lap controls, View menu, and result cards visible.]

This is Lap Results in Kestrel's Analysis Results tab. Once a lap has been analyzed, this is where I come to understand what happened and decide what to work on next.

A lap time tells me how fast I went. Here, I can find the sections that need attention, see where I gained or lost time, and compare my driving with an expert reference.

## Find a lap and read the results

[Show the track, car, and lap number beside the page controls. Use Previous or Next to move between analyzed laps, then settle on the demonstration lap.]

In Lap Results, each page contains the analysis for a baseline lap. I can move between them here and check which car, track, and lap I'm reviewing.

[Select All results in the View menu. Point to the frequency chart, then scroll to a numbered result card.]

The chart shows how often each label appears in the current view. Below it, the numbered cards show the individual results and their labels. I can scan these compact headers, then click one to open its comparison and section details.

## Choose what to focus on

[Change View to Mistakes, then Most common label in mistakes. Keep the reordered cards visible.]

I can narrow the view to mistakes, then bring the ones with the most common mistake labels to the top. This helps me spot repeated habits and choose a practice focus.

[Select Most time lost in mistakes. Keep the ranked cards visible. If recorded gap changes are unavailable, briefly show the option without claiming a measured loss.]

Most time lost in mistakes puts the largest recorded gap changes first, with results missing those values at the end. That helps me choose a section to investigate.

## Open a result and read the section timing

[Click a result header or its chevron to expand the card. Point to Section details and Lap time difference, then Section, Track position, Gap at entry, and Gap at exit where present. The comparison starts playing when opened; use Replay in the next step to show it again.]

Opening a card brings the comparison and Section details into view. Lap time difference shows the time gained or lost against the expert in this section: positive means time lost, negative means time gained, and zero means the gap stayed unchanged. The entry and exit gaps, when available, show how that gap changed through the section.

[If timing is missing, show Unavailable and No timing data for this section. If the comparison is missing, show Expert comparison unavailable; use another prepared card for the replay demonstration.]

## Watch the Driver vs Expert comparison

[In the expanded card, click Replay. Move the pointer away from the header and keep the card open. Show the Driver and Expert trajectories and the moving viewpoint.]

Here I can watch my driving alongside the expert reference. The view follows the movement through the section, with throttle, brake, and gear readouts where the data is available.

[Show the available Mistakes, Expert, and Recovery label groups. Where positioned labels are present, let their markers appear along the replay. Leave a few seconds without narration so viewers can follow the movement.]

The analysis labels help connect the driving behavior to the section. I can compare the two lines and inputs, then use Replay to watch again and decide what to try on the next lap.

[Let the status reach Replay complete. Click Replay to restart, then click the card header to collapse it.]

## Closing

[Reopen the demonstration card. Keep its labels and Section details visible, then end on the comparison replay.]

That's how I use Lap Results: choose a lap, find a section worth investigating, and compare my driving with the expert. I leave with one thing to practice on the next lap.
