# My Vision for Kestrel

Approximate runtime: 2 minutes  
Format: Founder-vision video with voice-over, racing footage, and conceptual product visuals  
Audience: Sim racers, coaches, teams, and potential partners

## 0:00–0:22 — The vision

**Visual**

Driving the car in the simulator.

**Voice-over**

Hardcord sim racing takes a long time to master, what if there is a way to visualize their performance, help them setup the car, manager racing strategies. This application brings data analysis accessible to casual players. I want Kestrel sim racing platform to make professional-quality  analysis approachable for beginners while delivering the tools for advanced drivers and teams. Other than serious data analysis. i would like to utilize the intelligence of the ai to do something fun. One little thing i already did is having ai to express with emojis. and my next target is moving over to iracing once I iron out the general strctures.
