# My Vision for Kestrel

Approximate runtime: 2 minutes  
Format: Founder-vision video with voice-over, racing footage, and conceptual product visuals  
Audience: Sim racers, coaches, teams, and potential partners

## 0:00–0:22 — The vision

**Visual**

Open on a driver preparing for a session. Blend racing footage with telemetry and Kestrel’s AI analysis environment.

**Voice-over**

My vision for Kestrel is to change the relationship between sim racers and their data.

Every session produces thousands of signals, but turning them into confident decisions should not require expert-level knowledge. I want Kestrel to make professional-quality motorsport analysis approachable for beginners while delivering the depth advanced drivers and teams need.

**On-screen text**

`Turn racing data into better decisions`

## 0:22–0:50 — An AI that understands racing

**Visual**

Show telemetry, track position, session events, and analysis views responding intelligently as the race develops.

**Voice-over**

At the center of that vision is an AI that does more than answer questions. It understands telemetry, recognizes the racing environment, and controls the analysis interface to bring the right evidence forward.

It should know whether the driver is practicing, qualifying, racing in traffic, managing a stint, or reviewing a completed session—and adapt its analysis to that context.

**On-screen text**

`An AI analyst aware of the complete session`

## 0:50–1:18 — A partner in driver development

**Visual**

Move across practice sessions, corner comparisons, driving patterns, setup development, and long-term progress.

**Voice-over**

I want Kestrel to become a genuine development partner. In practice, it should reveal where time is being lost, explain the driving behavior behind it, and turn that insight into a clear focus for the next run.

Over time, it should understand each driver—their strengths, repeated mistakes, habits under pressure, and rate of improvement across cars and tracks. It should also connect driver feedback with telemetry, helping teams make informed car setup decisions instead of relying on guesswork alone.

## 1:18–1:47 — Intelligence for racing

**Visual**

Show a live race with nearby opponents, changing conditions, stint trends, risks, and strategic possibilities.

**Voice-over**

During a race, I see Kestrel acting as a real-time strategic partner. It should understand pace, consistency, traffic, incidents, vehicle condition, and the behavior of nearby opponents.

The goal is not to overwhelm the driver with more information. It is to identify what matters now—where an opponent is vulnerable, when risk is increasing, how the stint is developing, and which decision creates the best opportunity.

## 1:47–2:00 — The destination

**Visual**

Bring together individual drivers, coaches, and teams using the same intelligence. Finish on the Kestrel logo.

**Voice-over**

Ultimately, my vision is for Kestrel to make every lap useful—not only for finding speed today, but for building a smarter, more adaptable driver over time.

That is the future I want Kestrel to create.

**End card**

`Kestrel Motorsport Analyst`  
`Every lap understood. Every session moving you forward.`
