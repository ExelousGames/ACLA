# Measured live iRacing telemetry

Captured: 2026-09-15T09:47:43.156412-04:00; 12.00 seconds; read-only shared memory.

- Car: BMW M2 CS Racing; track: Lime Rock Park, Grand Prix; session: Offline Testing.
- SDK: 331 channels, 60 Hz advertised, 719 distinct snapshots, 59.92 observed ticks/s.
- 69 channels changed during the capture. A present or constant channel is not proof of a fresh physical measurement.
- Application requests 72 native channels; 66 are present.
- Application declares 100/240 standardized fields mapped; 77 were emitted across the captured samples.
- Mapped counts include static metadata and derived fields. Raw and standardized counts are different schemas.
- This check exercised the existing Python shared-memory reader and JavaScript adapter; it did not verify the running UI or recording writer.
- Availability is specific to this car/session. The car remained stationary in the pits; this capture does not establish on-track freshness for constant channels.

## Findings

- Active cockpit, offline testing, neutral, approximately 0 km/h and 950 RPM, 41.925 L fuel at the final sample. Air 25.56 C; track 46.67 C.
- Present driver channels include throttle, raw brake, clutch, steering, RPM, gear, brake bias, traction-control adjustment and ABS activity. Motion, force-feedback torque, four-corner shock deflection and velocity, engine temperatures, lap timing/progress, weather, pit services and per-car arrays are also exposed.
- The native table includes six-element time arrays described by the SDK as 360 Hz for steering torque, motion and suspension. Production currently requests the scalar channels; this diagnostic follows the existing reader, which keeps only the last sub-sample for countAsTime arrays.
- `Brake` remained 1.0 while `BrakeRaw` remained 0.0. This demonstrates why the app uses the raw pedal channel for coaching; simulator-applied braking differs from the driver's input.
- All four `*brakeLinePress` channels were present but stayed at zero. iRacing explicitly documents that live brake-line-pressure output is zeroed, while disk telemetry retains it. These are not usable live brake-pressure measurements. [Official release notes](https://support.iracing.com/support/solutions/articles/31000170128-2023-season-3-release-notes-2023-06-05-02-).
- Cold/set tire pressures, carcass temperatures and wear were present and unchanged. Do not classify their presence as verified continuous on-track tire telemetry. The four hot `*pressure` channels were absent.
- `dcABS` and `dcTractionControl2` were absent; `BrakeABSactive` and `dcTractionControl` were present. ABS activity and an adjustable ABS setting are different data.
- Extra native channels not requested by the app include oil temperature, precipitation/wetness, incident counts, pit-repair timers, car-left/right status, per-car timing and suspension velocities. Availability alone does not establish equivalence to the app's standard fields.
- No native latitude/longitude, per-wheel slip or wheel-load channels were found in this table. Lap distance/progress and motion channels are present.
- An adapter mapping gap was observed: session metadata says `Offline Testing`, but the session-type converter accepts `Testing` and does not recognize `Offline Testing`, so `Graphics_session_type` is omitted. No production code was changed during this inspection.
- Invalid or unavailable lap data is omitted: `LapCompleted` was -1, no valid reference lap was established, and the short stationary capture cannot establish full-lap fuel consumption, sector times or traffic gaps.
