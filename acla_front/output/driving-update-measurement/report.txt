WHAT THE CURRENT CODE DOES
For accumulated per-sample gradients g_i and N samples:
  mean = sum(g_i) / N
  clipScale = min(1, gradientNormLimit / ||mean||_2)
  delta = max(actorRate, criticRate, demonstrationRate) * clipScale * mean
Defaults and this checkpoint: max rate = 0.001, gradientNormLimit = 1.
Therefore ||delta||_2 <= 0.001, before per-weight guards and floating-point rounding.
Relative actor/critic/demonstration rates are already incorporated into the gradients.
AI: learnSequenceAsync accumulates many targets and commits once; steps counts each target.
Human: learnDemonstratedPlanAsync averages available future plan targets into one origin loss;
each origin increments steps once and applies one update. This probe used all 31 future slots.
trainBatchAsync itself only coordinates completion/checkpoints; it is not an averaging optimizer.
The 69,703 saved steps alone cannot reveal historical update counts or the AI/human split.

LIMITATIONS
- Not a historical replay. Original map, speed reference, camera timing, controller ownership, sampled policy noise and action credits are absent.
- lap-proxy uses the recorded full lap as a surrogate racing line at assumed resolution 1000 and its speed as both reference and target; it is not the original centerline.
- reference-ablated zeros centerline and reference/target speeds. Both use recorded telemetry and video with zero alignment offset and 10 Hz resampling.
- Shift targets and shift gradients are masked: telemetry does not preserve paddle edges.
- AI uses eight sampled observations over four seconds, seeded policy exploration, five equally credited plan slots, and engineered advantages +1/+5. These are controlled sensitivity tests, not recorded rewards.
- Every measured case starts at the supplied checkpoint. Cap sweeps reuse the same gradient. Same-target loss changes do not measure held-out or closed-loop driving performance.
- A recording with mixed controller ownership cannot identify which frames were human demonstrations.
  The demonstration probes therefore treat selected recorded control trajectories as candidate labels.
- Resampled video pixels and timing need not equal the exact live capture pixels/timestamps.
- Current source hashes are saved. The files do not identify the source revision that originally trained the checkpoint.

INTERPRETATION
Confirmed: the cap binds in these probes, and it suppresses controlled AI gradients especially strongly.
There is numerical headroom for larger demonstration updates, but this is not a validated tuning change.
To decide whether it is too restrictive in use, record post-update diagnostics by controller mode
(sample count, pre-clip norm, clip scale, update norm, completed updates) and compare unchanged
learning rates at caps 1 and 5 on the same held-out recording and controlled simulator evaluation.
Keep update counts and sample counts separate; do not infer convergence from Learning steps.
