## Definition

Fallback label for a broken defense whose specific defense-type
signature cannot be read from the player's trace. Player attempted to
hold position against a close primary opponent and the opponent got
through - signed longitudinal gap flipped negative → positive. Prefer
a specific subtype when the trace identifies the defense - broken
inside cover or broken defensive lift on straight - and only fall
back to this label when neither matches.

## Solution

- Pick the one or two corners on the lap where an inside cover line
  actually rewards the defender; don't cover elsewhere.
- Break the tow on the straight (a small lift / lift-and-go feint)
  before the brake zone, not inside the corner.
- If the opponent is already alongside at the brake board, give the
  place cleanly and set up the undercut on the next corner instead of
  fighting through and losing two places.
